Gameplay Overview

Why Players Love Color Cauldron

An addictive puzzle game designed for all ages. Simple to pick up, challenging to master.

Simple Yet Strategic

Pour liquids from tube to tube until all tubes contain a single, uniform color. Simple touch controls let you play with one hand anytime!

Mystical Alchemy Themes

Unlock vibrant cauldrons, colorful spell potions, unique glass flask shapes, and enchanting sound effects as you progress.

No Time Limits or Stress

Enjoy smooth gameplay without stressful timers. Restart levels at any time or use helpful boosters whenever you get stuck.
